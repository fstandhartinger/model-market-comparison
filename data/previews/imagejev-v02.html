New · browser-use pilot

From seeing the screen to taking action

First measurements on controlled, original interfaces. One-step grounding and multi-step task completion are reported separately.

EXPLORATORY · NOT A LEADERBOARD

1Observe

The model sees a screenshot and a task. It does not receive the hidden answer, DOM selectors or browser state.

2Act

The harness validates the model's action and applies it to the controlled interface. Every attempt remains in the trace.

3Verify

The final application state determines success. A correct click and a completed workflow are different measurements.
