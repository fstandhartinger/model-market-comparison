## Findings that prevent acceptance

1. **R3.1 / P4 — open.** The live hero still says “the only place that shows what each model
   really costs you.” Artificial Analysis also exposes a per-model Cost per Task. The filtered
   provider-route and measured-efficiency distinction may justify narrower wording, but the
   exclusive claim is not supported by the retained comparison evidence.

2. **R5.3 — open.** Both live hosts load the Simple score slider at exactly `85`. The
   authoritative requirement says the default must be greater than 85. The pass-6 decision to
   interpret this as `≥85` is a conflict with the verbatim wording and cannot be marked verified
   by this gate.

3. **R9.1 — open.** The current `/api/meta` says selected feeds are dated 2026-09-13, but also
   reports provider/catalog feeds dated 2026-09-08, `provider_meta` dated 2026-07-12, coding-agent
   feeds dated 2026-09-08/09 and ECI dated 2026-09-12. “Every live source dated today” is not met.

4. **F-50 — open, larger UI defect.** The structure is correct on both hosts, but the explicit
   desktop acceptance check fails in both themes: the collapsed right-column content ends at
   1483 px while the radar ends at 1761 px, a 278 px gap against the ≤120 px bound. The opened
   state also overshoots the bound. This needs a Fable re-spec/implementation; no threshold was
   reinterpreted here.

5. **E2/E3 — open.** CursorBench is the only newly accepted slice and remains source-labelled,
   separate score/cost identities, with no invented joins. The dirty Vals/Frontier additions
   have no complete independent critic coverage: Vals has no critic receipts; Frontier has an
   unreviewed score batch and all cost rows unreviewed. The dirty collection plan also fails the
   registry-coverage guard. No such values were staged or trusted.

6. **P2/P3, X3/X4, X6 and X7 — remain open or in progress.** The PRD is independently accepted,
   but the Artificial Analysis capability gaps and positioning decision remain unresolved. F-51
   is now independently verified; F-50, R3.1, R5.3, R9.1 and E2/E3 still block the design and
   completeness gates. X6 has no passing line-by-line audit, so X7 must not send the final
   acceptance Telegram.

7. **Pending addendum work — not accepted.** The uncommitted `02-ADDENDUM-HERMES-CHAT.md` and
   `RELAYMODELS.md` request RelayModels wiring and per-run log verification, but `bin/pick-engine.sh`
   has not been wired for that provider. The brief simultaneously says this workstream must not
   call GPT-6 Astra; this gate therefore did not invoke the provider or treat the dirty request
   as shipped.
