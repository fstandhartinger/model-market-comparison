CR-213 asked for the line to cross incomplete models and for the box to be checked by default, so this is intended. But the "Most attractive quadrant" now highlights a frontier that no 7/7 model reaches, and the y-axis shows 100 for a 1/7 composite. Suggest the design authority decide whether the frontier should be computed from 4/7+ composites only, or the line should be styled differently for hollow points. The legend already says "labelled N/7".
2. **Chart label collisions (CR-11.3 acceptance).** Desktop: the "Claude Sonnet … · 1/7" label is truncated and crossed by the green line. Mobile (390 px): "Most attractive quadrant" sits on the line and its ring, and the "Claude Opus 5.5 ··1/7" label overlaps its ring (`charts-m-dark.png`, `charts-d-light.png`). CR-11.3 requires no overlap with data, so this is not verified.
3. **CR-222 label vs privacy text.** The footer changed from "visits" to "visitors", labelled as more accurate. The value is Umami's all-time `visitors` stat (`lib/umami-public-counter.mjs`, `startAt: 0`). `app/privacy/page.tsx` states that the salt rotates daily, so visitor figures count daily sessions and do not link a visitor across days. The number is therefore a sum of daily sessions, not people, so "18,000 visitors" can overstate unique people. Suggest wording such as "visits" or "daily visitors", or a privacy-consistent tooltip.
4. **Ledger gap.** `PROGRESS.md` has no rows for CR-210, 211, 212, 213, 216, 217, 218, 220, 222, 223, 225, 226 or 227, and only one mention each of CR-219 and CR-221. They are tracked only in PRs and job state, so a future acceptance gate cannot count them. Someone who owns those CRs should add rows, and they should stay `implemented` until independently verified.
