## Findings — claims that do not hold (flipped to `open`)

1. **H1 — history does not cover the scores Florian named.** Retained states
   (`data/raw/benchmarks/history/states/`, 2 states, both 2026-09-12) contain only the
   75-entry benchmark registry (69 with results). The AA Intelligence Index, AA Coding Index,
   Epoch ECI, DesignArena Elo and the Composite inputs are not in the registry and are not
   retained anywhere. Florian's literal example — "Opus 4.7 drops out of the AA Intelligence
   Index" — is exactly what iteration 6 did to *GPT-6 Astra (Non-reasoning)* via an approved
   AA withdrawal (`716a2b0`): the row is removed and its index values are not kept.
2. **H2 — no multi-hop bridging.** `crossVersionEstimates` and `datedEstimates` only bridge
   *directly* between one source and the current target via models present in both. There is
   no chain over intermediate snapshots/versions ("über eine Stufe oder über mehrere
   Stufen"), and no test for it. Also missing: bridging for the AA/ECI/Elo headline scores
   (depends on H1). What exists is solid (median ratio + IQR gate, rank shift for Elo,
   refusal causes, Composite recompute-required) and 12 tests cover it.
3. **B3 / B4 — missing scores bias the Benchmaxxing tag.** `scoreBenchmaxxing` is called with
   `minMeasured = 4` and no coverage floor; the score is the mean of within-topic adjacent
   jumps, so a model with 1–2 jumps gets a high-variance score. Recomputed on the shipped
   dataset (574 scored, 58 tagged): tag rate is **11 %** for models with <3 jumps, **12.7 %**
   for 3–7, but only **5.9 %** for 8–19. 28 of 58 tags rest on ≤2 jumps; *Claude 4.1 Opus
   (Reasoning)* is tagged on **one** jump, *o1-preview* on 7/214 axes. Publicly labelling a
   named model "Benchmaxxing signal" on that evidence contradicts B3 ("missing scores must not
   bias the result; explicit suppression when coverage is too thin").
   *Recommended fix (next iteration):* require ≥ 6 within-topic jumps spread over ≥ 2 topics
   before a score is published, and rank by a shrunken score
   (`n/(n+k)·mean + k/(n+k)·catalogMean`, k≈4) or by the lower bound of a bootstrap interval,
   then re-check that tag rate no longer falls with coverage. Add that check as a test.
4. **B7 — partially.** The existing test proves zig-zag > smooth specialisation on a synthetic
   profile, and domain specialisation is reported separately — good. But jumps are taken in
   *alphabetical* axis order within a topic, and percentiles compress at the top of the
   field, so well-covered frontier models are structurally less likely to be flagged than
   mid-field ones. Stays `implemented` with this critique; revisit together with B3.
5. **R8.1 — mobile page overflow.** Iteration 5 checked `/compare` only after selecting two
   models. At the default state the category cards were 429 px wide on a 390 px phone
   (page `scrollWidth` 466). `/benchmaxxing` overflowed too (model `<select>` 567 px, radar
   column 420 px; `scrollWidth` 612). **Fixed in this review** (see below); R8.1 therefore
   stays `implemented`, not `verified`, because the fix is new.
6. **P4 — the headline claim over-reaches.** The hero now reads "Every benchmark result for
   every model, and what each one actually costs." That is a stronger superlative than the
   "most complete collection" Florian allowed and than the numbers underneath support (75
   benchmarks, 6 without a single result; ECI/AA indices outside the registry). Reword so the
   claim is literally true (e.g. "the benchmark results for every model, in one place") —
   Fable 5.1 pass (X3) should pick the final line.
7. **X5 — docs lag.** `API.md` documents ECI, but `CHANGELOG.md` has no entry for E1 (ECI in
   the Composite, a scoring change downstream consumers must know), the derived top-20
   featured set (R4.4) or the `/compare` snapshots (R8.1). `MSG-UPSTREAM-SYNC-PROMPT.md` was
   last touched 2026-09-11 and mentions neither `data/raw/openrouter-data-policy.json` nor
   `data/raw/epoch-eci.json`.

  ≥ 85, featured-only and measured-tokens defaults and shows "6 models · filtered". A first-time
  Advanced user sees six rows of an 839-model catalog. (R5.1 intent; iteration 4 had to
  relax three filters to get 70 rows.)

  chips to two rows, and the Simple shortlist starts below the fold (only the score slider
  label is visible). Florian: "die Kernaussagen zuerst".

  design question: the page should lead with the models people actually compare.

  lever (`FEATURED_TOP_N` / default 85) is Florian's call, already carried in the X7 notes.
- `test/benchmax.test.mjs` carried byte-identical duplicate tests (3 copies of one, 2 of
  another) and three more same-named tests with different bodies — removed the identical
  copies; the same-named-but-different ones still deserve distinct names.
