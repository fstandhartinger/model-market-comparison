# CR-112 — correct djev openness in JevBench v1.2.11

This metadata-only release corrects our earlier claim that djev was closed and only planned to open.

- `Davipar/djev-dev` is David Villalón's Apache-2.0 runtime for Maisa's djev.
- It applies a structured, one-step inference method to Google's Apache-2.0 `diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it` weights and adds no separately trained djev weights.
- It is self-hostable; Maisa's hosted API, account and billing service remain separate.
- `mmastrac/djev-spark` is another DiffusionGemma structured-read runtime, optimized for NVFP4 on DGX Spark, not an independent model/checkpoint.
- All measurements, scores, ranks and public-task outcomes are unchanged from v1.2.10.

The three artifacts are pinned from public JevBench tag `v1.2.11` by SHA-256.
