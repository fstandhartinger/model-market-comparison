## The answer to the reply: the tariff was right, the label was not

docs.typesafe.ai/models, read 20 Sep 2026, verbatim: "Jev 1.13 | jev-1.13.0 | Price (per Btok / per Mtok) **$42 /
$0.042**" and "Price: Charged per input token. **Output tokens are free.**" That is exactly the tariff every Jev number
in JevBench has used since v1.0 (`price_input_per_m: 0.042`, `price_output_per_m: 0.0`). The blog's "Input tokens: from
$0.20 to $10 / MTok, output ~5x more expensive" is the LLM column of a two-column comparison; the Jev column right
beside it is "$0.042 / MTok, output FREE". The Google snippet ran the two columns together.

Our Cost column is dollars per **1,000 decisions**. One decision is a whole question — state, rubric and options — which
for Jev is 950 input tokens on average over the 534 v1.2 decisions. 950 × 1,000 × $0.042 / 1,000,000 = **$0.0399 per
1,000 decisions**. The reply read that as a per-token price, which the old label made far too easy.

## But checking every row found three arithmetic mistakes of our own

Repo: github.com/fstandhartinger/jevbench, tag `v1.2.3`, `results/v1.2/cost-correction-v1.2.3.json` (per-row figures and
derivations), `tests/test_cost_correction.py` (9 tests that stop them coming back).

1. The v1.1 and v1.1.3 cost averages used a row list holding the 242-decision standard+judge run **twice** (once as the
   standard tier, once as the judge tier) and the 72 easy decisions once — 556 rows instead of 314.
2. Rows priced from the gemini-3.1-flash-lite token counts used that run's standard+judge-only average (452 input tokens
   per decision) for all 314 v1.1 decisions, instead of its average over all 314 (383).
3. Requests whose answer came back unparseable were left unpriced although the provider billed them (HTTP 200, tokens
   generated). Only DeepSeek V4.1 Flash had any: 9 of its 314.

No tariff was wrong, no measurement, item or answer changed, **no rank changed**. Every metered tariff was re-read from
its primary source on 20 Sep 2026 and still matches: OpenAI gpt-5.6-luna $0.20/$1.20 standard, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite
$0.25/$1.50 paid, DeepSeek Flash $0.15/$0.60 cache-miss off-peak, djev $0.035 input / output free.
