that PR #22 "does not touch the SEO route files"; nobody checked the reverse direction, that the SEO
PR touches the live board head that F-193 had just tuned.

**Cause of the second step.** CR-158 puts the Jev-class Capability ranking and the bubble charts
*before* the JevBench board, so the board's first bar now sits 4,070 px down on a phone. Measured on
the live page at 390 px: the new lead ranking starts at 508 px and **its own first row is at
1,008 px** — so the "actual shortlist on screen" intent is unmet by the new headline board as well,
not merely relocated (`f189-super` measurements, this gate).

`data-bh-jev14-rank-by` in all four contexts; CR-158 deleted `components/JevRankBy.tsx`. This one is
*not* a loss of function: CR-151's "View by" switch supersedes it, and the live page does carry the

verified here at both widths. The pass-35 selector is stale, the page is not.

**Decision.** `F-189` and `F-193` both go back to **open**. The verifier was not weakened and the
