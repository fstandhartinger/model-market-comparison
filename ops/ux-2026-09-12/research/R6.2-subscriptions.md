# R6.2 — May a COMPANY use consumer AI subscriptions for employees?

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Question:** May a company (a business buying seats for employees) use the vendors' *consumer* AI subscriptions, or do vendors require a business/enterprise plan or API access?

**Method & rules followed:** Only official vendor domains (openai.com, anthropic.com, google.com, github.com, cursor.com, x.ai, plus their subdomains). No blogs, no Reddit, no news, no AI summaries. No numbers invented or extrapolated; unknown = "not published" / "could not fetch". robots.txt respected; no access control or paywall was bypassed — where the vendor's bot protection refused a plain fetch (Cloudflare on openai.com and x.ai), the page is recorded as **could not fetch**. One page (one.google.com) required a normal browser to render its USD prices; no login or paywall was involved.

> Currency notes: (1) Google prices for Google AI consumer plans were captured in USD from the US-locale page as rendered in a real browser. (2) The Google Workspace pricing page geo-serves EUR to this client (Finland) and did not serve USD; Workspace prices below are therefore recorded in EUR as published, with the USD-unavailability noted. (3) All other vendor prices below are USD as published.

## 2. Anthropic — Claude Pro / Max (vs Claude Team / Enterprise)

- **URLs read:**
  - https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms (read in full; this is the current consumer ToS, effective 2025-10-08, EEA/Switzerland consumer version served from this location)
  - https://www.anthropic.com/pricing (read in full)
- **Verbatim quotes:**
  > "These Terms apply to you if you are a consumer who is resident in the European Economic Area or Switzerland. You are a consumer if you are acting wholly or mainly outside your trade, business, craft or profession in using our Services."

  > "**Non-commercial use only.** You agree that you will not use our Services for any commercial or business purposes and we and our Providers have no liability to you for any loss of profit, loss of business, business interruption, or loss of business opportunity."

  Also noted in the same terms regarding what the commercial agreement covers:
  > "Please note: Our Commercial Terms of Service govern your use of any Anthropic API key, the Anthropic Console, or any other Anthropic offerings that reference the Commercial Terms of Service. For clarity, this does not include Claude.ai or Claude Pro use for individuals or entities."

- **List price per seat/month (USD):** Pro "$17 — Per month with annual subscription discount ($200 billed up front). $20 if billed monthly." Max "From $100 — Per month". Team: Standard seat "$20 — Per seat / month if billed annually. $25 if billed monthly." (Premium seat $100/$125.) Enterprise: "$20/seat. Usage cost scales with model and task."
- **Published quota:** **Not published in absolute numbers.** Only relative multipliers: "Every plan has usage limits that reset on a rolling five-hour session window… Pro gives you at least 5x more usage per 5-hour session than Free. Max gives you 5x or 20x more usage per 5-hour session than Pro." No message/token counts are published.
- **Verdict:** **companies not allowed** — Claude Pro/Max consumer subscriptions are consumer-only: the consumer terms restrict use to persons acting outside their trade/business and state "Non-commercial use only." (Caveat: quoted text is the EEA/Switzerland consumer-terms version served from this location.) Businesses are routed to Team/Enterprise or the API under Anthropic's Commercial Terms.

## 3. Google — Google AI Pro / Ultra (vs Gemini for Google Workspace)

- **URLs read:**
  - https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/ (FAQ read; USD prices captured from the browser-rendered US-locale variant https://one.google.com/intl/en_us/about/google-ai-plans/)
  - https://workspace.google.com/solutions/ai/ and https://workspace.google.com/pricing.html (geo-served in EUR only)
- **Verbatim quote** (FAQ on one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans):
  > "Only personal Google Accounts can sign up for Google AI plans. Workspace customers can sign up for a Gemini add-on to their existing subscription."

- **List price per seat/month (USD):** Google AI Plus $4.99/mo; Google AI Pro $19.99/mo; Google AI Ultra "From $99.99/mo" — plan comparison cards: Ultra 5x $99.99/mo, Ultra 20x $199.99/mo (US-locale page as rendered in browser).
- **Business-side price:** Workspace Business Standard €13.60/user/month (annual commitment; promo €6.80 for first 3 months as published) — **this client's location is served EUR; USD price not served**. Workspace Enterprise: "Let's talk" (price not published).
- **Published quota:** **Not published in absolute terms** for Gemini usage (relative multipliers only: "2x*", "4x*", "5x Pro plan", "20x Pro plan"). Numeric items the page does publish: Google Flow Credits 200/1,000/10,000/25,000 per month by tier and storage (400 GB / 5 TB / 20 TB / 30 TB).
- **Verdict:** **companies not allowed** — the vendor's own FAQ states only personal Google Accounts can sign up for the consumer Google AI plans; business/work accounts must use the Workspace/Gemini offering instead.

## 4. GitHub — Copilot Individual (vs Copilot Business / Enterprise)

- **URLs read:**
  - https://github.com/features/copilot/plans (plan prices + FAQ)
  - https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/plans
  - https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing-and-usage/individuals/billing
  - https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing-and-usage/organizations-and-enterprises/seats-and-billing-cycles
- **Verbatim quotes:**
  > "GitHub Copilot Individual is designed for individual developers, freelancers, students, educators, and open source maintainers. The plan includes all the features of GitHub Copilot Business except organizational license management, policy management, and IP indemnity." (github.com/features/copilot/plans)

  > "GitHub Copilot Business … Made for organizations an enterprises, this plan offers centralized management and Copilot policy control for organization members." (docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/plans — "an enterprises" sic)

  On the economics of the separation:
  > "If you have an active Copilot Pro, Copilot Pro+, or Copilot Max plan, and are then assigned a seat as part of a Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise plan, your personal Copilot plan will be automatically canceled." (docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing-and-usage/individuals/billing)

  On prices for organizations:
  > "GitHub offers the following plans for organization accounts:
  > * **Copilot Business** at $19 USD per user per month, includes 1,900 AI credits per user, and access to a broad model catalog.
  > * **Copilot Enterprise** at $39 USD per user per month, includes 3,900 AI credits per user (GitHub Enterprise Cloud only), and priority access to new models and features."
  (docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing-and-usage/organizations-and-enterprises/seats-and-billing-cycles)

- **List price per seat/month (USD):** Pro $10; Pro+ $39; Max $100. Business $19 per granted seat/month; Enterprise $39 per granted seat/month ("Copilot Business $19 USD per granted seat per month", "Copilot Enterprise $39 USD per granted seat per month").
- **Published quota:** **Yes.** Free: 2,000 completions/month. Paid individual plans publish total monthly AI credit allowances: Pro $15-equivalent total (1,000 base + 500 flex), Pro+ 7,000, Max 20,000 AI credits/month; Business 1,900 and Enterprise 3,900 AI credits per user/month; 1 AI credit = $0.01 USD.
- **Verdict:** **unclear from published terms** — GitHub positions Copilot Individual/Pro as "designed for individual developers" bought on a *personal* GitHub account, and reserves org license/policy management, IP indemnity, and org-wide pooled usage for Business/Enterprise; no explicit prohibition of employees using an individual plan at work was found in the published pages read (the Copilot FAQ even contemplates companies firewallow-controlling access to individual plans: "you can use your network firewall to explicitly allow access to Copilot Business and/or block access to Copilot Pro or Free.").
