Comparison Summary
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average, however fairly concise. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) scores 47 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 24). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 90M tokens, which is fairly concise in comparison to the median of 90M.
Pricing for GPT-5.6 Sol (max) is $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $10.00). In total, it cost $3464.84 to evaluate GPT-5.6 Sol (max) on the Intelligence Index.
At 58 tokens per second, GPT-5.6 Sol (max) is slower than average (65).

When was GPT-5.6 Sol (max) released?

Who created GPT-5.6 Sol (max)?

How intelligent is GPT-5.6 Sol (max)?
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) scores 47 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other reasoning models in a similar price tier (median: 24).
How fast is GPT-5.6 Sol (max)?
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) generates output at 58.5 tokens per second (based on OpenAI's API), which is below average compared to other reasoning models in a similar price tier (median: 65.2 t/s).
What is the latency of GPT-5.6 Sol (max)?
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) has a time to first token (TTFT) of 156.54s (based on OpenAI's API), which is at the higher end compared to other reasoning models in a similar price tier (median: 3.75s).
How much does GPT-5.6 Sol (max) cost?
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) costs $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (at the higher end, median: $10.00), based on OpenAI's API.
What is GPT-5.6 Sol (max) API pricing?
GPT-5.6 Sol (max) costs $4.00 per 1M input tokens and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (based on OpenAI's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $3.08 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider.
