How to read a “new model just dropped” screenshotName the version. EQ-Bench 3 ≠ 4. Vending-Bench ≠ Vending-Bench 2 ≠ Arena. Terminal-Bench 2.1 ≠ 4.0. Aider Python ≠ polyglot.
Name the harness. SWE-bench and Terminal-Bench move 10–20 points with Codex vs Claude Code vs a custom scaffold.
Self-run ≠ official. Aider, OTIS, lm-eval, WeirdML local loops, and homebrew 128k needle tests are posted the same hour as the launch card and often disagree later.
Judge boards are taste boards. EQ-Bench CW, Lech Mazur, Caliper, RP-Bench, Arena Elo measure preference / style / slop, not GPQA.
Willingness boards are policy boards. UGI W/10 and Caliper C-rates measure refusals, not intelligence.

If you want a short “watch this tab” list for the next drop: Arena Elo, SWE-Verified, Terminal-Bench (named version), HLE, LiveBench, Aider polyglot, EQ-Bench 4 + Creative Writing v3 + Slop, SimpleBench, UGI, Fiction.liveBench, WeirdML v2, Vending-Bench 2, Caliper, Lech Mazur writing.

Separately, otis at runotiss.com is a small personal coding/agent harness: N parallel sandboxed tries, a real shell/Python verifier, pass@k curves, otter theming. It is not a public multi-model board and is easy to confuse with the math set. If someone posts “OTIS 94%” next to AIME, they mean the mock AIME. If they post a pass@k plot from an otter site, they mean the harness.
